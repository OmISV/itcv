Yogapeeth Kids

YogaPeeth Activities for development of spiritually strong Kids

Yogapeeth is one of the multi-disciplinary and more vigorous types of yoga that strengthens and empowers the body, mind, and spirit. Kids are energetic and sportive in every endeavor. The practice of yoga nidra aims to teach students to communicate with their bodies and access their spiritual inner values so they can achieve the best results in their academic career.
